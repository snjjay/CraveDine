# Module 1 - Deal/Offer Management & Redemption Workflow

## Objective

Implement an EatClub-style Deal/Offer and Redemption workflow where:

- Restaurant Owner creates Deals/Offers.
- Customer redeems Deals/Offers.
- Restaurant completes the redemption after payment.

---

## Business Workflow

### Step 1

Restaurant Owner creates a Deal/Offer.

### Step 2

Customer redeems the Deal/Offer by selecting:

- Arrival Date
- Arrival Time
- Guest Count

Status:

Redeemed

### Step 3

Customer arrives and informs the restaurant that they redeemed the Deal/Offer.

Restaurant verifies the conditions.

### Step 4

At payment time the restaurant enters the bill amount.

System automatically calculates:

- Discount Amount
- Final Amount

Status becomes:

Completed

## Business Rules

- Restaurant Owner creates Deal/Offer.
- Customer redeems Deal/Offer.
- Restaurant Owner completes redemption.
- Discount is calculated on the server.
- Customer cannot complete redemption.
- Completed redemption cannot be completed again.

## Interview Questions

### Why calculate discounts on the server instead of the client?

Because client values cannot be trusted.

---

### Why separate Deal from Redemption?

A Deal is reusable.

A Redemption is a single customer transaction.

---

### Why use Status?

To control the business workflow.

Redeemed

↓

Completed

↓

Cancelled

↓

Expired
